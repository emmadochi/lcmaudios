## 3. UI/UX Architecture Breakdown

### 1. Intent-Driven Discovery Interface
* **Spiritual State Selector**: Top horizontal filter bar allowing instant switching between *Morning Devotion*, *Deep Worship*, *Warfare Prayers*, and *Study Focus*.
* **Hero Content Cards**: Prominent daily devotional audio tracks and curated sermon series.

### 2. Adaptive Audio Player Experience
* **Persistent Bottom Mini-Player**: Uninterrupted playback across all screens with simple expand/collapse swipe gestures.
* **Synchronized Lyrics & Notes Overlay**: Real-time transcript tracking with highlightable sermon notes and quick timestamped social sharing.

### 3. Library & DRM Offline Management
* **Offline Access Telemetry**: Clear visual status indicators for DRM-protected local downloads.
* **Community & Church Playlists**: Dedicated space for personal favorites, custom collections, and shared church community playlists.
